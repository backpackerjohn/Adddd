# Winner: Recoup (team adhd-tax)

*Decision memo by the contest chair, published verbatim. The conditions below are binding on the product spec.*

DECISION: Recoup (team adhd-tax) wins.

## How the decision was made

The panel totals put adhd-tax first (193), ignition second (190), wildcard third (181), temporal fourth (177), evergreen fifth (143) — a 3-point gap at the top, well inside judge noise. So the scores did not decide this contest; the attacks did. Both finalists took three declared "kill" attacks. The question the chair is obligated to answer is not "who scored highest" but "whose thesis is still recognizably itself after the attacks land." On that test the outcome is not close.

## Why Recoup survives narrowed rather than gutted

**The core AI claim was never touched.** Across all three attacks on Recoup, no attacker disputed the load-bearing capability: open-world multimodal triage of an unbounded long tail of documents plus bespoke recovery drafting (waiver letters, payment-plan requests, appeals) is genuinely impossible pre-LLM, impossible with integrations, and priced out of reach as a human service. Two judges scored it 9/10 on AI necessity; the tech judge called it "the only thesis where the LLM does work that was literally impossible before." The attacks went after the trigger (capture), the reward cadence, the safety gates, and the P&L — the periphery, not the organ.

**The evidence base is the strongest in the field and the attackers implicitly conceded it.** Recoup sits on the only 0.8-confidence skeptic verdict and the only cluster with objective national-registry outcomes (Science Advances: 6x credit default by age 40) rather than self-report and TikTok. No attack disputed the pain, the unserved remainder (physical mail, fines, returns, the shame loop), or the willingness-to-pay logic of a product whose ROI is a visible dollar number.

**Every kill line came with an adoptable fix — and most fixes were already latent in the thesis.** The retention attack's strongest demand (automatic ingestion so the loop doesn't bootstrap from the avoider photographing the avoided object) is an acceleration of features Recoup already scoped: email forward-in is in month one, USPS Informed Delivery and partner mode were in months 2-6. The clinical attack's demands (never auto-dismiss legal/government mail, deadline-critical items exempt from the one-move cap and self-silence, zero-retention privacy, RSD-safe send) are constraints on the design, not replacements of it. The market attack's demand (stop pretending to be an engagement-subscription business; price episodically) changes the invoice, not the product. The surviving version of Recoup is Recoup with better plumbing, harder safety rails, and honest pricing. The conditions below make all of it binding.

## Why T-Minus is gutted, not narrowed

I want to be honest: T-Minus is the sharpest single product idea in this contest. "A frozen person can't open an app, but almost anyone can answer a ringing phone" is the best sentence any team wrote, and the behavior judge ranked it first for exactly that reason. But the three attacks on it — all at 0.78 confidence, the highest in the round — converge on the same joint from three directions, and that joint is the only joint the product has:

1. **The mechanism**: habituation happens at the ring, not inside the call. All of T-Minus's anti-habituation machinery (infinite script variability, live negotiation) lives behind the answer gate; the stimulus the user actually decides on — same contact, same ringtone, same windows — is the identical-repeated-stimulus profile its own evidence says goes invisible. The Wakie precedent (1.5M users, pivoted away because daily calls "became a little intense") and the IVR literature (24% complete-answer rates; compliance worst in younger adults) are direct empirical analogs the thesis never surfaced. The target persona sits in the documented telephobia cohort, and iOS Sleep/Work Focus silences the flagship 7:15am and 10am calls by default.
2. **The economics**: measured realtime-voice pricing puts a retained heavy user at roughly $25-60/month COGS against a $12 price. Negative gross margin that scales with success, with auto-pause billing removing the only cushion. This attack is arithmetic, not opinion.
3. **The ethics**: the "zero shame surface" claim is architecturally false — PSTN delivery writes a permanent missed-call ledger into the OS's most prominent app, and the efficacy/harm fork (the call only works via social pressure, which is the RSD wound) has no answer in the thesis. No crisis floor, no fading protocol, reckless transcript posture.

Critically, the surviving versions the attackers could offer — B2B2C through coaches at $50-150/mo, or a prepaid 8-12 week bridge program, or SMS-first — are different companies. T-Minus's own thesis concedes SMS-first is "a different, weaker product" and lists no fallback identity. A thesis whose narrowed form is unrecognizable did not survive narrowing. That is the definition of gutted.

## Why not the un-attacked theses

Wildcard (Callie, 181) inherits every ignition kill at higher dose: 3-5 calls/day spends the answer-reflex budget faster, the month-one scope is smeared across four loops, and the thesis omits carrier spam-flagging entirely — the operational risk T-Minus at least planned for. Its genuinely brilliant contribution (four skeptic-conceded gaps share one shape: an agent that initiates contact and carries context) is a strategic insight, and I graft it below rather than crown it. Temporal (Pacenote, 177) has the contest's best failure-mode architecture but rests its flagship value claim on the one mechanism its own citations say has zero formal research (waiting mode), while fighting free pre-installed OS features; its judges' scores already price that in and it still finished fourth. Evergreen (Molt, 143) is the most intellectually honest submission and the weakest company: its load-bearing bet (content novelty defeats channel-level habituation) is contradicted by the pre-attentive nature of reflex-swiping, its own risk list concedes the telemetry can't detect its own failure, and its lane is the most saturated shelf in the market.

## What the attacks legitimately cost Recoup

The win is conditional, and the conditions are not cosmetic. The market attack landed on the P&L: Cushion's death with 200k paying customers is real evidence that consumer-aligned fee recovery at a flat monthly fee struggles at scale, and Gmail's free AI bill-surfacing compresses the email half of the wedge. Recoup's answer must be what the attacker's own surviving version specifies: revenue coupled to delivered recoveries, ingestion that doesn't depend on sustained user behavior, and the recovery-playbook corpus (per-creditor waiver/appeal success rates) built deliberately as the defensible asset — the one moat Gmail, Rocket Money, and a weekend GPT wrapper cannot copy. The retention attack landed on the honeymoon-jackpot problem: the six-week backlog is a one-time reward spike, and steady-state recoverable events for the employed persona may be quarterly. That is why the success metrics in the conditions abandon week-6 engagement as the KPI in favor of dollars-recovered-per-user-year and reactivation-per-envelope-event — making the JMIR cliff irrelevant rather than pretending to beat it. The clinical attack landed on the false-negative liability and the confirm-UI complacency problem; both are now hard product law. The capture-behavior bet remains the top open risk and stays as the week-1 kill-metric test with a week-4 re-capture measurement, because the clinical attacker is right that the decoupling insight may be one-shot.

Recoup wins because it is the only finalist whose attackers, at their most lethal, were describing a better version of the same product. T-Minus's attackers were describing its obituary.

## Grafts adopted from losing theses
- From wildcard (Callie): the inbound-trigger insight. Once automatic ingestion (Informed Delivery, forward-in email, virtual address) learns of a dollar event, Recoup pushes it outbound — SMS or a short call — so a user dark for five weeks still hears 'your ticket doubles Friday: $85 → $170' without ever opening the app. The agent initiates contact and carries the context; the user never has to travel to the tool.
- From temporal (Pacenote): the custody-handoff ritual and reliability receipt. When an item enters Recoup, the UI performs an explicit transfer of vigilance ('This envelope is mine now — watching 3 deadlines'), and a boring, locally computed receipt counter (deadlines watched / deadlines never silently missed) accumulates as the trust asset. Also adopted wholesale: Pacenote's rule that AI writes words but a deterministic scheduler pulls every trigger — no date or dollar figure ever passes through a generative step after human confirmation.
- From ignition (T-Minus): the deterministic miss-protocol discipline. Recoup's notification ladder for deadline-critical items is hard-coded and promised in advance at onboarding (one escalation, one partner-failover ping if configured, then documented silence), never model-discretionary, and never guilt-framed.
- From evergreen (Molt): the falsifiability dashboard and honest self-silencing. Recoup instruments its own primary claims — capture rate at weeks 1/4, dollars recovered per user-year, reactivations per envelope event — against a pre-registered kill threshold, and when it silences a channel it says so out loud ('these aren't landing; I'll wait for your next snap') rather than shouting into a dead channel.
- From wildcard (Callie): the consented household tier as an early expansion path — a partner can hand Recoup an item ('the forms are due Friday') so the agent, not the spouse, carries the nag, monetizing the dyad documented in the research without making the partner the police.

## Binding conditions on the winner
1. AUTOMATIC INGESTION IN MONTH ONE: USPS Informed Delivery integration, a Recoup mail-forwarding/virtual-address option, and partner/household bulk-capture mode ship in month one alongside camera capture and email forward-in. The loop must be able to start and restart with zero sustained user behavior; camera capture is a convenience, never the sole trigger.
2. LEGAL-MAIL FLOOR: 'Requires nothing from you' verdicts are permanently restricted to commercial/marketing mail. Government, tax, court, legal, insurance-EOB, and debt-collection documents always surface for human eyes, and this scope limit is stated in marketing and onboarding. Deadline-critical items are exempt from both the one-move-a-day cap and the 3-ignores self-silence rule.
3. ANTI-COMPLACENCY CONFIRMATION: for any money- or deadline-bearing item, confirmation requires active transcription (the user types the deadline date/amount read from the shown source region), not a passive approve tap. A tap-to-approve flow for consequential extractions is prohibited.
4. PRIVACY AS PRODUCT LAW: contractual zero-retention or on-device processing for document images; no training on user documents; per-document consent; published deletion policy and breach duty. The 'documents rot on counters, so sensitivity is low' rationale is struck from all reasoning and materials.
5. REVENUE COUPLED TO RECOVERY, NOT ENGAGEMENT: offer a per-executed-recovery transaction price (flat dollar fee, never a percentage of savings) alongside or instead of the flat subscription; auto-pause billing stays. North-star metrics are dollars recovered per user-year, reactivations per scary-envelope event, and week-4 re-capture rate — week-6 engagement is explicitly not a KPI.
6. RSD-SAFE SEND AND FRAMING: Recoup relays creditor denials neutrally under its own agent identity where lawful ('the issuer said no this time; second asks succeed ~40%'); default notification framing is recoverable-dollars (gain), with loss-framed countdowns opt-in; no push notification may ever quantify an already-missed loss.
7. PRE-SCALE KILL TEST: before paid acquisition, run the 20-user mail-pile pilot measuring (a) week-1 capture rate and (b) week-4 re-capture after the first dollar event — testing whether the camera becomes the envelope. Pre-register the failure threshold; if capture collapses, automatic-ingestion channels become the primary product before any further consumer spend.
8. MOAT INVESTMENT: the per-creditor recovery-playbook corpus (waiver grant rates, appeal outcomes, payment-plan acceptance by issuer) is instrumented from the first user and treated as the company's defensible asset; the app is the wrapper, the corpus is the moat.
9. SCOPE PROHIBITIONS STAND: never move money, never hold payment credentials, no credit-repair or clinical claims, positioned as an adjunct scaffold with an optional household escalation contact for bad-EF weeks — not a standalone fix.

## Why the others lost
- **ignition**: Gutted, not narrowed. Three 0.78-confidence attacks converge on the single load-bearing joint — the answered ring — from mechanism (habituation happens at the stimulus, Wakie and IVR analogs, telephobic cohort, OS Focus modes suppress the flagship calls), economics ($25-60/mo measured voice COGS against a $12 price, negative margin scaling with success), and ethics (zero-shame claim falsified by the OS call log; the efficacy/harm fork has no answer; no crisis floor). The only surviving versions are different companies (B2B2C via coaches, prepaid bridge program, SMS-first), and the thesis itself concedes SMS-first is 'a different, weaker product' with no fallback identity. Best single idea in the contest; not a survivable consumer thesis as submitted.
- **wildcard**: Inherits every ignition kill at higher dose: 3-5 daily calls spends the answer-reflex budget roughly twice as fast, the highest price ($25/mo) meets the most burned buyer, month-one scope is smeared across four half-proven loops, and the riskiest-assumptions list omits carrier spam-flagging entirely — a disqualifying blind spot for a product that IS outbound calls. Its genuine contribution — four skeptic-conceded gaps share the inbound-agent shape — is grafted into the winner rather than crowned.
- **temporal**: The contest's best failure-mode architecture (deterministic triggers, AI-written words) wrapped around a flagship claim its own citations say has zero formal research: if waiting mode is attentional dominance rather than alarm distrust, the guardian gets Maya to the dentist but never returns her morning, collapsing the product to a premium leave-on-time notification competing with free pre-installed OS features. Fourth on points before any attack was even run; the receipt/custody mechanics are grafted into the winner.
- **evergreen**: Lowest panel total (143) and the field's least-supported load-bearing bet: that message-level novelty defeats notification habituation, when the documented reflex-swipe is pre-attentive and channel-level — the LLM writes copy nobody reads. Its own risk list concedes per-user telemetry (~5 events/day) cannot distinguish tune-out from a busy week, so the engine's failure is invisible to its own dashboard. Most saturated shelf in the market, weakest wow, hardest invoice to justify. Its falsifiability discipline and honest self-silencing are grafted into the winner.
